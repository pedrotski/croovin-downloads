# Croovin downloads

Official desktop installers for [Croovin](https://play.croovin.com/discover/), a music player for your Plex library.

## Mac preview

[Download Croovin for Mac](https://github.com/pedrotski/croovin-downloads/releases/download/v0.1.0-preview.1/Croovin-0.1.0-preview.1-mac-universal.dmg)

macOS 13 or later. One universal installer for Apple Silicon and Intel.
Open the DMG, drag Croovin to Applications, and launch it from Applications.

This is an early preview. It is ad-hoc signed, **not Developer ID signed or notarized by Apple**. macOS may block the app or require approval in its normal Privacy & Security settings. Do not disable Gatekeeper. You can use [Croovin in your browser](https://play.croovin.com/) at any time.

Requires your own Plex music library and an internet connection. Your browser login is not imported; sign in once in the desktop app. Closing the Mac window keeps music playing; Cmd+Q quits.

This repository contains installers, checksums and release notes only. Application source is maintained separately. Windows direct downloads and iOS/Android store apps are planned, not available yet.
